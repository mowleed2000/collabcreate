FAQs

Common questions

What does Collab Create Marketing actually offer?

CCM is a business solutions company with a broad commercial portfolio — including digital and AI solutions, business utilities and connectivity, payments and financial solutions, and branded products — plus a sales partner programme for people who want to develop opportunities across the UK.

Are you a traditional marketing agency?

We retain a strong digital practice (websites, demos, reviews, growth and acquisition systems), but the wider business is built around helping companies explore practical commercial solutions across multiple categories.

Do you sell energy or broadband yourselves?

We help businesses review arrangements and explore available market options. Provider availability and products depend on the enquiry, location and eligibility. Named brands on our service pages are presented as market context, not automatically as exclusive contracted suppliers.

How do sales partners work with CCM?

Partners may work with structured guidance as sales representatives or more autonomously as independent partners — introducing businesses to relevant products and submitting opportunities through our process. Arrangements are commission-based and discussed during onboarding.

How do I start?

Businesses can explore Business Solutions or contact us. Prospective partners should visit Sales Partners or apply for the Sales Representative role.
