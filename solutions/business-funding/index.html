Business Funding

Explore funding options for working capital, equipment or expansion.

Businesses may be able to explore finance products for cashflow, equipment or growth plans — subject to eligibility, credit criteria and what is available at the time of enquiry.

Use cases

Where funding conversations often start

Working capital

Smooth cashflow gaps between paying suppliers and receiving customer payments.

Equipment

Acquire tools, vehicles or kit that unlock capacity without draining reserves.

Expansion

Support growth plans where a structured finance product may be more suitable than ad-hoc borrowing.

CCM does not guarantee approval or specific products. Outcomes depend on eligibility, documentation and lender or provider criteria.

What an enquiry typically involves

Share business context

Information you may need

Recent management figures or accounts, bank statements, details of existing credit, and a clear description of how funds would be used. Exact requirements vary by product.

FAQs

Questions about business funding

Is funding guaranteed if I enquire?

No. Enquiries start a review process. Approval depends on eligibility and available products.

How fast can funding be arranged?

Timelines vary by product and completeness of information. We avoid promising fixed turnaround times that cannot be controlled.

Can startups enquire?

Some products favour established trading history. Share your stage in the enquiry and we can indicate whether suitable pathways exist.
