FAQs

Questions about card payments

Should I chase the lowest rate only?

Rate matters, but settlement, reliability, hardware costs and contract flexibility can outweigh a small fee difference.

Do I need new hardware?

Not always. It depends on your current setup and the products being considered.

How long do contracts typically last?

Terms vary widely. Understanding your remaining commitment is part of a responsible review.
