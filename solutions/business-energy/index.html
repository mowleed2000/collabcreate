FAQs

Questions about business energy

Can you guarantee lower bills?

No responsible adviser should guarantee savings. Outcomes depend on market rates, consumption, contract timing and product eligibility.

What documents should I prepare?

Recent energy bills, meter details if available, contract end dates and a brief note on how the premises are used (hours, seasonal patterns).

Do you only work with certain suppliers?

We use market-options framing. Specific products discussed will depend on what is available and appropriate for your enquiry.
