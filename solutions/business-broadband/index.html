FAQs

Questions about business broadband

Do I need “business” broadband specifically?

Many SMEs prefer business products for support hours, SLAs and usage terms. Suitability depends on criticality of uptime and how you use the line.

Can you install at my address?

That depends on network coverage and technology at the premises. Share your address in an enquiry and we can explore available options.

Will switching interrupt service?

Changeovers need planning. We treat migration timing as part of the conversation, not an afterthought.
