The role

A serious B2B sales campaign — not a side hustle pitch

As a Self-Employed Business Sales Representative with Collab Create Marketing, you identify prospects, approach business owners, generate opportunities, qualify needs, present relevant products from the portfolio, follow up, and develop your own pipeline.

You operate with flexibility on when and where you work, while using CCM training materials, scripts and product guides to sell professionally across categories.

Product portfolio

What you can introduce to businesses

Websites & digital

Demos, premium sites, reviews, growth and acquisition systems.

AI voice agents

Call handling and lead-capture automation conversations.

Business energy

Electricity and gas arrangement reviews.

Card payments

Processing fees, hardware and contract discussions.

Broadband

Connectivity options for SMEs and independents.

Branded cups & funding

Printed products plus business loans/funding pathways subject to eligibility.

Responsibilities

Day-to-day activities

01

Prospect & approach

Build lists, open conversations with decision-makers and create new opportunities in your territory or channel.

02

Qualify & present

Diagnose needs and present the portfolio categories that fit — without forcing irrelevant products.

03

Follow up & submit

Maintain pipeline discipline, submit leads through CCM’s process and stay engaged through progression.

Candidate profile

Who thrives in this role

✓

Self-motivation

Comfortable generating activity without a salaried safety net.

✓

Commercial curiosity

Able to learn multiple product categories and match them to real business needs.

✓

Professional communication

Clear spoken and written communication with business owners.

✓

Resilience

Support

Training, scripts and product guides

Representatives receive structured materials to accelerate product knowledge and conversation quality — including scripts and guides across the portfolio. Support is designed to help you sell competently, not to invent results on your behalf.

Working arrangement

Self-employed. You control your diary within the expectations of a professional sales partner. Tax and national insurance are your responsibility as a self-employed individual.

Earnings

£35,000–£120,000 OTE · Commission only · Uncapped

OTE figures describe potential earnings based on performance. They are not a salary, not a guarantee and not a promise of minimum income. Actual earnings depend on your activity, skill and successful opportunities.
