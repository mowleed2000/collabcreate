FAQs

Partner questions

Is this employment?

The Sales Representative role is self-employed and commission-only. Independent partners also operate commercially under agreed terms — not as salaried employees unless separately stated in writing.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. OTE figures on the representative role describe potential earnings based on performance. Actual income depends on activity, skill and closed opportunities.

What support is available?

Representatives can access training, scripts and product guides. Independent partners receive commercial onboarding focused on portfolio knowledge and submission process.

How do I apply?

For the representative role, use the dedicated careers application. For independent partner interest, use the contact form with the Sales Partner category.
